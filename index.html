Leny's Makera_NC Editor

Load a program and park the cutter where the job should resume.

  1. Open an .nc file, or drop it on this window.
  2. Play the path, drag the slider, or use Prev lift to park on a retract.
  3. Save. Makera_40_redo.NC is the job from 40% onward.

      Draw on the top view. The shape is only a drawing until you press Apply. Check tools in the job list to cut just those, or leave Tools on All tools.

        Shape 1

        Save from playhead

        The number in the name is the percent this file continues from. It selects that tool, starts the spindle, lifts to clearance, crosses to the restart XY, feeds down, then runs the rest of the path. The Z1 may pause for a tool change before it moves.

        Space plays. Arrows nudge. Drag to pan, and drag in Iso to orbit.

        Thread hole 1

        The hole and the thread are two different cuts, with two different bits. The drill only makes a plain pilot hole. After that, the machine changes tools. A thread mill, which is smaller than the pilot, drops into the center and spirals around to cut the grooves. Do not try to cut the thread with the drill bit.
